A little context

Hello, I'm Taylor.

Developer, careful listener, and believer that the web should work well for everyone.

The short version

I like the space between a good idea and a useful thing.

For the last eight years, I've helped small teams turn ambitious ideas into clear, durable web experiences. I enjoy untangling the hard parts: understanding what people need, finding the simplest path through a screen, and making the details feel considered.

My process is collaborative and hands-on. I ask questions early, share work in progress, and keep accessibility and performance in the conversation from day one, not as a final polish.

Outside work, you'll find me on a neighborhood trail, trying a new recipe, or rescuing a houseplant from my own overconfidence.
