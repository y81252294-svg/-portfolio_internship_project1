Making it easier for neighbors to find a plot, share a harvest, and grow together.

Small business · 2024

Goodform

A clear, welcoming toolkit that helps independent makers get paid on time.

For a small-business support nonprofit, we simplified a resource-heavy experience into a concise set of practical steps. The result puts plain language first and works just as well on a phone as at a desk.

A more inviting front door for an independent arts and events space.
